Privacy Policy — Personal Health Sync

Personal Health Sync is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • What is accessed: read-only access to the owner's own Google Health data (sleep, heart metrics, activity, profile).
  • How it is used: daily summaries are written to the owner's private spreadsheet for personal health tracking.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
